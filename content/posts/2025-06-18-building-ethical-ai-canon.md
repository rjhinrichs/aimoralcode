What makes a value “canonical” in AI ethics?

Drawing from over 43 seed values and thousands of ethically coded phrases, the latest AI Moral Code update operationalizes **Trust**, **Justice**, **Privacy**, and **Autonomy** through a rigorous computational lens. Each candidate is analyzed through:

- **TF-IDF Stratification**
- **Sector Normalization**
- **Contextual Multipliers**
- **Composite Value Scoring (CVS)**

The result is a living architecture of values—relevant, testable, and grounded in real-world ethical use cases. An **Ethical Salience Tracker (EST)** will soon allow iterative recalibration of the Canon based on legislative, semantic, and cultural shifts.

This marks a transition from *ethical theory* to *value telemetry*—placing normative fidelity at the center of machine governance.
