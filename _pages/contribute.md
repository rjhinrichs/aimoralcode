# 🤝 Contribute to the AI Moral Code

We welcome principled collaborators to shape the ethical scaffolding of AI systems. Whether you're an educator, developer, researcher, or policy architect, your insights help operationalize values in meaningful, auditable ways.

## 📝 Submit a Blog Post

Use our [blog post template](https://github.com/rjhinrichs/aimoralcode/issues/new?template=new-blogpost.yml) to share case studies, reflections, or corpus findings. All contributions are reviewed for ethical alignment, clarity, and reproducibility before publication.

## 📜 Value Definitions and Canonical Mapping

Want to propose new ethical values or refine existing ones? Submit your ideas using the [Value Proposal Issue Template](https://github.com/rjhinrichs/aimoralcode/issues/new?template=value-proposal.yml). Please provide:
- Clear value name and definition
- Domain relevance (e.g., education, healthcare)
- Empirical references or corpus excerpts
- Suggested alignment category (Normative, Regulatory, Behavioral, Conceptual)

## 🔍 Semantic Drift Detection

If you've observed frequency shifts or semantic drift in your own corpora, we invite you to submit a Drift Insight Issue with:
- Corpus metadata and timestamps
- Affected values and definitions
- Audit logs or cosine score trails (Excel, Python, etc.)

## 💬 FAQs

**Q: Who reviews submissions?**  
A: A distributed team of ethical AI researchers led by Professor Ran Hinrichs, guided by empirically grounded and value-curated practices.

**Q: What happens after I submit?**  
A: Contributions are reviewed, annotated, and incorporated into our live ethical infrastructure with full attribution.

**Q: Is there a code of conduct?**  
A: Absolutely. We expect contributors to embody transparency, humility, and ethical intentionality in all interactions.

Thanks for helping make AI systems more principled, interpretable, and aligned with human values.
