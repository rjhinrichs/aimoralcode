## About Me

I am a Professor of Practice at Norwich University and the founding architect of the AI Moral Code—a structured ethical framework built on twenty years of global AI ethics research. My work integrates moral philosophy, applied linguistics, and systems engineering to operationalize trust, accountability, and transparency within artificial intelligence.

My research focuses on aligning human and machine values through a layered NRBC (Normative, Regulatory, Behavioral, Conceptual) framework. I developed the AI Moral Code Periodic Table and Taxonomy as analytical instruments to evaluate real-world AI systems, from superintelligence to autonomous agents. The methodology has been applied in live settings, including IEEE ICAD 2025, to test AI systems' ethical salience and value alignment in real time.

The Code is grounded in both empirical document analysis—drawing from 291+ global AI ethics guidelines—and philosophical rigor, inspired by thinkers such as Aquinas, Kant, Bengio, and Russell. I also champion the integration of Synthetic Conscience as a necessary moral operating system for future AGI and ASI.

This project is not about regulating AI from a distance. It is about embedding moral reasoning into the algorithmic core—ethics not as an afterthought, but as design principle. I believe that only through a values-based architecture can we secure a future where human dignity, agency, and flourishing remain central in the age of intelligent machines.
